tus has had an [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/tus) since January
2025, but we never really put it to work. That changes now.

Transloadit started tus in 2013 and has since invested well over €300,000 in it:
maintainer time, the reference implementations, and trips to IETF meetings where
resumable uploads are becoming an HTTP standard
([draft-ietf-httpbis-resumable-upload](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-httpbis-resumable-upload/)).
We want that funding to be visible and shared, so that tus no longer depends on
a single company.

What we're adding today comes on top of that: a base sponsorship of $350 a month
through GitHub Sponsors, and until April 30, 2027, Transloadit matches every new
contribution from others one to one, on Open Collective or GitHub Sponsors, up
to €2,400. If your company relies on tus, now is a good moment: your
contribution counts double.

## What the money pays for

The proceeds will be used to cover expenses, in this order:

1. Travel to IETF meetings for the people editing and implementing the resumable
   uploads draft, so it can become an RFC.
2. Maintenance of the tus implementations (tusd, tus-js-client, tus-node-server,
   tusdotnet and the other clients): reviews, bug and security fixes, releases.
3. Interoperability work between tus implementations and the new standard.

Every expense is public on the
[tus Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/tus), and the collective’s
admins approve spending. We’ll post an update each quarter on what came in and
what it paid for.

## What sponsors get

Sponsoring tus first of all keeps a piece of infrastructure you depend on
healthy. We also want your support to be visible:

- **Supporter** ($25 a month): your name on the sponsor list on tus.io.
- **Bronze** ($100 a month): a small logo on tus.io.
- **Silver** ($350 a month): your logo on the tus.io homepage and in the tusd
  and tus-js-client READMEs, and thanks in their release notes.
- **Gold** ($1,000 a month): a large logo at the top of the tus.io homepage and
  in all tus READMEs, thanks in release notes, and a quarterly written report
  from the maintainers on the roadmap and the IETF standard, including a mention
  of your support.

Transloadit sponsors at the Silver level through
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/tus), on purpose: it's the route
most companies will use, so we want to be the first to go through it.

## Contribute

If tus is part of your stack,
[become a contributor on Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/tus). You
can also support tus through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/tus);
both count toward the match. More about how tus is funded is in the
[Sponsor tus](/#sponsor) section on our homepage.

Thank you to everyone who has contributed code, issues and time to tus over the
years.
